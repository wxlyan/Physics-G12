Kinematics

Motion in One Dimension

What is Kinematics?

Kinematics is the branch of Physics that studies the motion of objects without considering the forces that cause the motion.

Key Quantities

• Distance

• Displacement

• Speed

• Velocity

• Acceleration

Important Formulas

v = u + at
s = ut + ½at²
v² = u² + 2as

Example

A car starts with velocity 10 m/s and accelerates at 2 m/s² for 5 seconds.

v = u + at

v = 10 + (2 × 5)

v = 20 m/s

Quick Quiz

If u = 5 m/s, a = 3 m/s² and t = 4 s, find v.
