What is Dynamics?

Dynamics is the branch of Physics that studies the relationship between motion and the forces affecting it.

Key Concepts

• Force

• Mass

• Acceleration

• Newton's Laws

Important Formulas

F = ma
Weight = mg

Example

A 5 kg object is pushed with a force of 20 N. Find the acceleration.

F = ma → a = F/m = 20/5 = 4 m/s²
