Momentum

Linear Momentum & Impulse

What is Momentum?

Momentum is the product of mass and velocity of an object.

Key Concepts

• Momentum (p)

• Mass (m)

• Velocity (v)

• Impulse (J)

• Conservation of Momentum

Important Formulas

p = mv
Impulse = FΔt
p_initial = p_final (Conservation)

Example

A 2 kg object moves with velocity 5 m/s. Find its momentum.

p = mv
p = 2 × 5
p = 10 kg·m/s

Momentum Flow

🏎 Object → m·v → Momentum → Force / Time → Impulse

Momentum = Mass × Velocity
Impulse = Force × Time
