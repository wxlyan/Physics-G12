What is Work?

Work is done when a force causes an object to move through a distance.

Key Concepts

• Work

• Energy

• Power

• Kinetic Energy

• Potential Energy

Important Formulas

W = Fs
KE = ½mv²
PE = mgh
P = W/t

Example

A force of 20 N moves an object 5 m. Find the work done.

W = 20 × 5
