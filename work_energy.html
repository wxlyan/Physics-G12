Work, Energy & Power

Energy Transfer and Power

What is Work?

Work is done when a force causes an object to move through a distance.

Key Concepts

• Work

• Energy

• Power

• Kinetic Energy

• Potential Energy

Important Formulas

W = Fs
KE = ½mv²
PE = mgh
P = W/t

Example

A force of 20 N moves an object 5 m. Find the work done.

W = Fs
W = 20 × 5
W = 100 J

Work • Energy • Power

💪 Force ↓ 📦 Object ↓ ➡️ Motion ↓ ⚡ Work Done ↓ 🔋 Energy Transfer ↓ 🚀 Power Output

Work = Force × Distance
Energy = Ability to do work
Power = Rate of doing work
