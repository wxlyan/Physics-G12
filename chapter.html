Chapter 1 : Kinematics

📘 Chapter Summary

Kinematics is the study of motion without considering the forces causing it.

🎯 Key Concepts

  • Distance
  • Displacement
  • Speed
  • Velocity
  • Acceleration

📐 Important Formula

v = s / t
a = (v - u) / t
s = ut + ½at²
